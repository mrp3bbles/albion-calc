Make more silver

  • Buy orders beat instant buy. Place buy orders for hide and fiber at night and refine the next day. That's often the whole difference between a loss and a profit. Switch "Buy via" to Buy order to see it.
  • Refine in the bonus city. Leather in Martlock and cloth in Lymhurst return 36.7% of materials instead of 15.2%.
  • Daily bonuses. When the game shows a +10% or +20% refining or crafting bonus, enter it under Settings → Daily bonus. It noticeably raises your return rate.
  • Don't flood the market. Selling 500 of something that sells 100 a day crashes the price. The planner caps each item at a share of daily sales; spread big batches across days or cities.
  • Check for spikes before you commit. Tap any item's icon to see 30 days of prices. If today is a spike, the profit won't last until your sell orders fill.
  • Eat a salad before crafting. Bean / Turnip / Potato Salad give +33 / +66 / +99 crafting quality and craft faster. Add one from a craft session's shopping step.
  • Journals are extra profit. Fill a crafting journal while you craft and sell it full. Add journals in a craft session and they count toward profit.

Keep the numbers honest

  • Start the Albion Data Client first, then Albion. It only uploads markets you open while it's running, so launching it first means nothing gets missed.
  • Keep the Albion Data Client running while you play. Every market you open gets uploaded, so the cities you trade in get fresh prices, for everyone.
  • Watch the Age column. Red or "no data" means a guess. Open that market in-game (the data client uploads it), hit Refresh, or type the price into the cell.
  • Max price age (top bar) hides stale prices, so scans don't chase a week-old listing.
  • Check the station fee once and enter it under Settings. The station shows it before you craft.
  • Log every run. Expected profit and real profit are different things. After a week, the Run log shows which runs are worth your evening.

Spec & focus

  • Without focus, refining spec doesn't change profit; it only makes focus cheaper later. Level it anyway, since refining gives the XP.
  • Once you have premium, turn on Focus and sort by Silver/focus. Spend focus where each point earns the most, and refine T4 to push your spec.
  • Crafting spec raises your odds of better quality. After a few craft sessions you'll know your real odds; enter them under Settings → quality odds.

Safety

  • Don't carry more through red or black zones than you can afford to lose. Black Market flips end in Caerleon, so they carry that risk.
  • Your sync link works like a password: anyone who has it can edit your settings and log. A username and password is easier to type on a new phone.

Return rate: production bonus %

Return rate = 1 − 1/(1 + bonus%). Defaults: 15.2% royal city, 36.7% refining bonus city, 24.8% crafting bonus city, +focus on top.

Market %

Station

Fee = item value × nutrition factor × usage fee / 100. Item values come from the game files.

Focus efficiency (FCE)

Focus cost = base × 0.5^(FCE / 10000). Base focus costs come from the game files.

Crafting quality odds %

Sell value = these odds × each quality's price (falling back to the next-lower quality when it has no price). The default (100% Normal) is the safe assumption; raise the others once you know your spec's real odds.

City crafting bonuses

Each royal city gives +15% production (a better return rate) when you craft certain item types there. The crafting tab and sessions use this to pick your craft city. Faction capes, normal capes, bags and gathering gear have no city bonus. If the game ever shows one, set it below.
